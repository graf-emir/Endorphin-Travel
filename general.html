General Information

Visa

For UAE residents, visa requirements for Kyrgyzstan depend entirely on your passport nationality rather than your UAE residency status.
Check your specific passport eligibility directly on the official Kyrgyzstan e-Visa Portal.

Payment

The amount can be paid with following options:

1 Total payment at once
2 Two installments: The 1st Installment (non-refundable deposit) after booking and second installment six weeks before the trip.

The non-refundable deposit equals: 500$ per person
The last payment can be made in cash and is to be handed over upon arrival at the destination hotel.You will receive an email with detailed information about your payment scheme after your registration.
